# Page 6 of 6 — Story Page 5: A Letter Full of Love

## Page purpose

Deliver the emotional payoff: the village has noticed Milo’s everyday kindness. End with warmth and the sense that tomorrow will bring more small connections.

## Story moment

Milo opens the letter. His friends and neighbours gather to thank him for making Poppywick feel like home.

## Characters present

- **Milo:** Centre focus, seated or standing beside his red bicycle as he reads the open letter. His eyes shine, he wears a small joyful smile, and his ringed tail curls into a gentle heart-like shape.
- **Bunny:** Immediately beside Milo, presenting a flower garland. White fur, yellow apron.
- **Bear:** Behind and slightly to one side, holding a tray of honey biscuits. Cocoa fur, green hat with orange flower, green apron.
- **Mouse:** In the foreground on a small parcel tied with red string or on Milo’s bicycle basket. Grey fur, violet scarf, proud smile.
- **Hazel Hedgehog:** Holding a flower basket; coral-pink cardigan.
- **Della Duck:** One wing lifted in greeting; teal neckerchief.
- **Pip Squirrel:** Perched on a low crate with a paper-wrapped parcel; mustard vest and large russet tail.
- **Greta Goat:** At the back, slim and tall, carrying a covered tray of honey biscuits; berry-red shawl.

## Illustration brief

### Composition

Set the scene in the village square at golden late afternoon. Milo is seated on a low wooden bench or standing just before it at the centre of the lower half, holding the open cream letter at chest height. He is the only character looking down at the letter; every other face looks warmly toward Milo. Arrange the group in a loose protective semicircle so the image feels like a hug rather than a party crowd.

### Foreground action

The opened letter is readable enough to show the central thank-you phrase, but Milo’s face is the primary focal point. Bunny places or holds up a loose flower garland. Mouse stands proudly at Milo’s side with the small red-string parcel. Milo’s red bicycle leans nearby, wicker basket visible, and his cap remains on his head. His tail curls into a soft heart shape close to the ground.

### Midground and background

Bear’s biscuit tray, Hazel’s flower basket, Della’s lifted wing, Pip’s parcel, and Greta’s covered tray identify each supporting friend without creating mini-stories. Behind them, show the post office, two glowing cottage windows, a low bunting string, and the first blush of a peach-pink evening sky. Keep the village square open and uncluttered.

### Light, palette, and mood

Golden light with warm cream and peach highlights. Signature clothing colours remain clear against the soft village background. The mood is tender, grateful, and celebratory in a quiet, cuddly way—not loud or chaotic.

### Complexity limit

**Medium-high, controlled.** Nine named characters are present, but there is only one action: everyone is thanking Milo. Use clear spacing, distinct silhouettes, and no extra animals or moving objects. Milo and the letter must be found instantly.

## Exact page text

Milo opened the letter.

“Thank you for making Poppywick feel like home,” it said.

Milo’s tail curled into a heart.

“Then tomorrow,” he said, “I’ll bring even more smiles!”

## Text treatment and placement

- Put the thank-you sentence **on the open letter**, in large and highly legible handwriting. This is the message being read, not a speech bubble.
- Put the first and third lines in a narration block in the calm upper-left sky.
- Put Milo’s last line in one speech bubble near his face, leaving all other characters silent and smiling.

## Continuity check

- The envelope is now open; it remains cream paper with the red seal and the tiny matching paw print.
- Every named supporting character must use the exact defining clothing and colour from the character bible.
- Bear must be large but set back or bent slightly; Greta must be tall but not visually dominant; Mouse must remain clearly small.
- End with Milo’s navy cap, cream shirt, red satchel, and red bicycle still visible: he is ready for another kind tomorrow.
