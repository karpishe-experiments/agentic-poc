# Page 1 of 6 — Cover

## Page purpose

Introduce Milo, his postman identity, and Poppywick Village in one instantly readable image. This is a promise of warmth and movement, not a crowded catalogue of characters.

## Story moment

It is early morning. Milo is setting out on his route, and the village is waking up to greet him.

## Characters present

- **Milo:** Primary focus. Use his full character-bible design: ginger tabby with exactly three forehead stripes, green eyes, navy postman cap, cream shirt, and red satchel.
- **Bunny:** Small background/midground cameo at the bakery doorway. White rabbit, yellow apron, holding a loaf.
- **Bear:** Background/midground cameo in the garden. Cocoa-brown bear, green hat with orange flower, green overall apron, holding a watering can.
- **Mouse:** Tiny cameo in an upstairs cottage window. Grey fur and violet scarf must remain visible.

## Illustration brief

### Composition

Portrait full-page illustration. A curved cobbled lane begins wide at the bottom centre and leads into the village, creating a clear path for the eye. Milo rides his tiny red bicycle down the lane toward the viewer at a gentle three-quarter angle. He should occupy about one-third of the page height and be the only sharply focused character.

### Foreground action

Milo has just rung the brass bell with one paw while keeping the other on the cream handlebar grip. His red satchel is full but closed; a few colourful envelope corners peek from the flap. He wears a wide, soft smile and his ringed tail curls upward like a question mark. The wicker basket holds one small bunch of daisies, not letters.

### Midground and background

On the left, Bunny stands in the open, round-doored bakery entrance, waving with one paw and holding a golden loaf in the other. On the right, Bear waters a small patch of oversized daisies beside an apple tree. Mouse peeks from a second-floor round window above a flower box. Include two cream cottages, the bakery’s striped awning, a small red postbox, and low green hills beyond; keep these elements charming but subordinate to Milo.

### Light, palette, and mood

Fresh morning sunlight, pale blue sky, warm cream buildings, poppy red accents, leaf green foliage. The feeling is sunny, safe, and gently bustling.

### Complexity limit

**Medium.** One lead action, three small cameos, four to six environmental details. No extra named villagers, no floating letters, no visual gag competing with Milo.

## Text

**Title:** *Milo the Village Postcat*

**Optional tagline:** A little letter with a very big thank-you.

## Text placement

Put the title in the open pale-blue sky across the upper third. Keep the tagline small below it. Do not put body copy on the cover.

## Continuity check

- Milo’s bicycle is red, with wicker basket, cream grips, and brass bell.
- Milo’s cap, shirt, and satchel match the character bible exactly.
- Bunny, Bear, and Mouse are cameos only; Milo remains visually dominant.
