# *Milo the Village Postcat* — Character Bible

## Purpose

Use this document as the single source of truth for every written scene and illustration. Characters should look recognisably the same on every page, even when their pose, emotion, or distance from the viewer changes.

## Shared illustration language

- **Style:** Soft, hand-painted storybook illustration with rounded shapes, gentle texture, and expressive eyes. Characters are animal villagers who stand, gesture, and use objects like people, while keeping animal paws, ears, tails, and natural silhouettes.
- **Proportions:** All characters have large, expressive heads; compact bodies; short limbs; and small paws or hooves. Their heads are about as tall as their bodies. Avoid realistic anatomy or sharp angles.
- **Face and eyes:** Eyes are large and warm, with dark pupils, a clear highlight, and simple curved eyebrows. Keep noses small and soft-edged. A character’s ears and tail should help show emotion.
- **Scale:** Milo is the central reference size. Bunny is slightly shorter, Mouse is much smaller, and Bear is substantially taller and broader. Maintain these relationships in group scenes.
- **No changes between pages:** Do not alter a character’s species, fur colour, signature markings, eye colour, or signature clothing. Clothing may move naturally with the action but must retain its core colours and details.

## Milo — the village postcat

### Role and personality

Milo is the protagonist and Poppywick’s dependable postman. He is kind, thoughtful, quietly brave, and pleased to be useful. His curiosity is gentle rather than mischievous. He treats every letter as important and every neighbour as a friend.

### Species, build, and scale

- A young adult ginger tabby cat with a small, round, cuddly build.
- About the height of Bunny; slightly wider through the middle because of his fluffy fur.
- His large head and soft cheeks make him immediately appealing to preschool-aged children.

### Fur, markings, and face

- Base fur is warm marmalade orange with a pale cream muzzle, chin, chest, belly, and paw tips.
- Exactly **three** darker russet-brown stripes sit vertically on his forehead. These are his clearest identifying feature.
- He has subtle darker tabby bands around both front legs and his tail.
- His nose is a small dusty-pink triangle; his whiskers are short, fine, and dark brown, three on each cheek.
- His eyes are bright leaf green with large dark pupils and two tiny white highlights each.
- His ears are triangular and orange outside, pale peach inside; the left ear has a very small, harmless-looking notch near the tip.
- His tail is long, plush, orange, and ringed with four darker brown bands; its tip is dark brown.

### Clothing and props

- Navy-blue postman cap with a short curved brim. Centre front: a small round gold badge stamped with a paw print.
- Cream short-sleeved collared shirt, always visible beneath his satchel strap.
- Red canvas mail satchel worn diagonally from his right shoulder to his left hip. It has a brass buckle, rounded flap, and a small cream label patch with a red paw symbol.
- Tiny red bicycle: cream handlebar grips, a wicker basket on the front, and a small brass bell on the right handlebar. It is small enough for Milo’s feet to touch the ground when seated.

### Expression and movement guide

- **Happy:** Wide soft smile; eyes curve slightly; tail curls upward like a question mark.
- **Puzzled:** Ears angle forward; mouth makes a tiny “o”; one eyebrow lifts; tail makes a loose curve.
- **Thinking:** One paw rests beneath his chin; gaze tilts slightly upward; ears stay alert.
- **Touched or emotional:** Green eyes shine; smile is small and closed; tail curls near his feet or forms a gentle heart-like curve.
- Milo moves with care. When he carries a letter, he holds it neatly between both front paws.

### Consistency rules

- Always show the three forehead stripes, green eyes, navy cap, cream shirt, and red satchel unless the scene explicitly removes an item.
- Never make Milo a kitten, a large cat, or a different tabby colour.
- Keep his bicycle red and his satchel red; they are visual anchors throughout the book.

## Bunny — the village baker

### Role and personality

Bunny is Milo’s energetic, imaginative neighbour. She runs the little bakery and makes optimistic guesses during the mystery. Her warmth feels bright and bouncy.

### Species, build, and scale

- A small white rabbit, just a little shorter and narrower than Milo.
- Compact pear-shaped body, round cheeks, and very large upright ears.
- Her feet are slightly oversized and soft, helping her look springy and playful.

### Fur, markings, and face

- Snow-white fur with a faint warm-cream shadowing around the cheeks, belly, and ear edges.
- Large hazel-brown eyes with soft black lashes: two short lashes at the outer corner of each eye.
- Small rose-pink oval nose; pale pink inner ears; tiny pink paw pads when visible.
- A round cotton-white tail, visible whenever she is turned sideways or away from the viewer.

### Clothing and props

- Sunny yellow baker’s apron with a white scalloped trim and a small white pocket shaped like a loaf of bread.
- The apron ties in a neat bow at her back.
- When working, she may hold a wooden bread peel, a striped tea towel, or a loaf of golden bread. These are optional props, not permanent accessories.

### Expression and movement guide

- Bunny leans forward when excited, ears straight up and slightly apart.
- She gestures with both paws and often stands on the very tips of her feet.
- **Delighted:** Open smile, one ear tilts playfully to the side.
- **Thinking hard:** Ears bend inward at their tips; one paw touches her cheek.
- Her voice and body language should feel quick, hopeful, and friendly—not frantic.

### Consistency rules

- Keep Bunny white with hazel eyes, pink inner ears, and the yellow apron.
- Her ears should remain much longer than her head; never replace them with short rounded ears.
- Do not add a hat or other permanent clothing that competes with her apron.

## Bear — the village gardener

### Role and personality

Bear is the calm, gentle thinker of the group. He tends flowers and vegetables in his garden. His slow, careful way of speaking makes the other characters feel safe.

### Species, build, and scale

- A friendly brown bear, around one-and-a-half times Milo’s height and more than twice Milo’s width.
- Large and round with a broad tummy, wide shoulders, and soft, slightly bowed legs.
- His size reads as comforting, never intimidating; he often bends or sits to join the smaller friends at eye level.

### Fur, markings, and face

- Main fur is rich cocoa brown.
- His muzzle, eyebrows, chest patch, and inner ears are warm honey beige.
- He has dark amber-brown eyes, a broad charcoal-brown oval nose, and small rounded ears.
- A tiny leaf-shaped tuft of fur rises between his ears; it should appear in every clear view of his head.
- His paws are wide and rounded, with no visible claws.

### Clothing and props

- Moss-green gardener’s hat with a gently drooping brim and a small orange flower tucked into the hatband on the left side.
- A dark forest-green overall apron with two large front pockets. The apron may be partially hidden by his body in close shots but should be present when visible.
- Optional gardening props: silver watering can, small trowel, seed packet, or basket of apples.

### Expression and movement guide

- Bear moves slowly and deliberately; he crouches carefully so he never overwhelms the smaller characters.
- **Thoughtful:** One paw under his chin, brows gently lowered, mouth slightly pursed.
- **Happy:** Big crescent smile; eyes almost close; shoulders relax.
- **Surprised:** Rounded eyes and a tiny raised paw, never a dramatic or scary expression.
- His tail is not normally visible and should not become a prominent feature.

### Consistency rules

- Keep the cocoa fur, honey-beige muzzle/chest, green hat with orange flower, and green overall apron.
- He must always be clearly larger than Milo and Bunny.
- Avoid claws, teeth, or any bear-like feature that could look threatening.

## Mouse — the small, confident neighbour

### Role and personality

Mouse is the smallest member of the main group and the boldest with silly ideas. She is observant, expressive, and never treated as less important because of her size.

### Species, build, and scale

- A tiny grey field mouse, approximately one-third of Milo’s height.
- Small round body, delicate arms, round belly, and large ears that are almost as wide as her head.
- In group scenes, she may stand on a low stone, crate, bench, or bicycle basket so her face is visible.

### Fur, markings, and face

- Soft dove-grey fur with a pale silver-grey muzzle, belly, and inner ear edge.
- Large deep-brown eyes with bright white highlights.
- Little round pink nose, fine dark whiskers, and large blush-pink inner ears.
- Her tail is thin, smooth, pale pink, and about as long as her body. It curves behind her in an expressive S shape; keep it visible whenever practical.

### Clothing and props

- Violet-purple knitted scarf, wrapped once around her neck with one short end in front and one longer end drifting behind her.
- The scarf is her key identifier and remains on in every page.
- Optional props: a thimble-sized parcel, miniature notebook, or tiny rolled map.

### Expression and movement guide

- Mouse stands tall, puts paws on her hips, or raises one paw high when she has an idea.
- **Excited:** Big open smile; ears lift wide; tail makes a loose curl.
- **Curious:** Head tips to one side; paws clasp under her chin.
- **Proud:** Small closed smile and an upright, confident posture.

### Consistency rules

- Keep Mouse clearly tiny, grey, brown-eyed, pink-eared, and in her violet scarf.
- Her tail should be slender and pink, never fluffy like a squirrel’s.
- Do not make her childlike in capability: her ideas and expressions should be confident.

## Hazel Hedgehog — flower-shop neighbour

### Role and personality

Hazel is a cheerful supporting villager who joins the final thank-you gathering with flowers. She brings a gentle, practical warmth to crowd scenes.

### Physical design

- A small hedgehog, roughly the same height as Bunny but much rounder.
- Face, arms, and belly are warm cinnamon brown; her back is covered in short, rounded chocolate-brown spines with cream tips. The spines must look soft and non-threatening.
- She has shiny black eyes, a tiny dark-brown oval nose, and small round ears.
- Wears a coral-pink cardigan with two round cream buttons. The cardigan ends just above her round tummy.
- Carries a small woven basket overflowing with daisies, poppies, and cornflowers.

### Expression and movement guide

- Hazel smiles broadly and often holds her flower basket with both paws.
- Her spines remain smoothly rounded; do not flare them in anger or fear.

## Della Duck — pond-side neighbour

### Role and personality

Della is a sunny supporting villager who joins the final gathering. She is enthusiastic, social, and adds motion with her small wing gestures.

### Physical design

- A plump white duck, a little shorter than Milo, with a rounded body and short orange webbed feet.
- White feathers with a very pale blue-grey shadow under the wings and along the lower body.
- Bright orange bill and feet; large sky-blue eyes with two short upper lashes.
- Wears a teal-blue neckerchief tied in a small side knot beneath her bill.
- Holds a tiny sky-blue envelope or a small bunch of wheat tied with red ribbon.

### Expression and movement guide

- Della often lifts one wing in greeting or clasps both wings together when delighted.
- Keep her bill softly rounded and friendly; never give it a sharp, realistic profile.

## Pip Squirrel — orchard neighbour

### Role and personality

Pip is a nimble supporting villager who appears at the final gathering. He gives the crowd a playful, outdoorsy energy.

### Physical design

- A small russet-red squirrel, a little shorter than Milo and leaner than Bunny.
- Cream muzzle, belly, and chest; dark brown almond-shaped eyes; small rounded ears with subtle ear tufts.
- His defining feature is a huge fluffy russet tail, almost twice the length of his body, with a cream underside and darker brown tip.
- Wears a mustard-yellow vest with two tiny acorn-shaped wooden buttons.
- Carries a little paper-wrapped parcel tied with red string.

### Expression and movement guide

- Pip perches lightly on a low wall, crate, or tree root; his large tail balances behind him.
- His excitement is shown by a gently swishing tail and bright, eager eyes, not frantic motion.

## Greta Goat — hill-path neighbour

### Role and personality

Greta is a calm, affectionate supporting villager who brings baked treats to the final gathering. She adds a tall, graceful shape to the crowd without drawing focus from Milo.

### Physical design

- A cream-white goat, about Bear’s shoulder height but slim and gentle in build.
- Long oval face, soft floppy ears, small curved caramel-coloured horns, and a neat cream beard under her chin.
- Warm golden-brown eyes with long, kind lashes; rose-pink nose.
- Wears a berry-red shawl draped over her shoulders and fastened with a round wooden brooch.
- Carries a round tray of honey biscuits covered by a red-and-white checked cloth.

### Expression and movement guide

- Greta bends her long neck slightly to speak with smaller friends and smiles with a calm, closed mouth.
- Her horns are short and rounded. Never use pointed, dramatic horns or a stern expression.

---

## Background villagers and birds

Background animals should remain soft, simple, and secondary: an occasional robin, sparrow, chick, or distant rabbit may add life to Poppywick. They should not introduce a new recurring character, bright signature outfit, or competing story action. Use them to frame Milo’s journey, not distract from it.

## Group-scene placement and continuity checks

- **Scale order, tallest to smallest:** Greta (tall but slim), Bear (largest overall), Milo, Bunny, Hazel, Della, Pip, Mouse.
- Put Bear and Greta toward the back or bend them slightly in group scenes so Milo remains easy to find.
- Keep Bunny’s yellow, Milo’s navy-and-red, Mouse’s violet, Bear’s greens, Hazel’s coral, Della’s teal, Pip’s mustard, and Greta’s berry red distinct in the same image.
- Before finalising an illustration, check: Milo has three forehead stripes; Bunny has long ears and yellow apron; Bear has his green hat and orange flower; Mouse has her violet scarf; the tiny letter has its red seal and paw-print clue when the story calls for it.
