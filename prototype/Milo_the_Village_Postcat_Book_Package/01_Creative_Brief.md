# *Milo the Village Postcat* — Creative Brief

## Approval status

**Approved:** 18 July 2026

This is the fixed creative brief for all reference sheets, page prompts, illustrations, and final book layout. A change here affects every later production stage and should be made deliberately.

## Final deliverable

- **Book length:** Six images/pages total: one cover and five story pages.
- **Page format:** **10 × 8 inches, landscape orientation** (5:4 aspect ratio).
- **Final assembly:** The six completed landscape pages will be bound into one PDF. Print specifications are intentionally out of scope for now.
- **Text workflow:** Generate illustrations without embedded title text, narration, speech bubbles, or readable letter text. Typeset the approved story text after the artwork is final.

## Intended reader

Children aged 3–5, read aloud with an adult. Every image must be emotionally clear at a glance: one obvious action, expressive faces, and a safe visual place for text.

## Core visual direction

The supplied reference images establish a **luminous, richly textured, painterly storybook world**. The illustrations should look handcrafted and atmospheric—not flat vector art, glossy 3D animation, or photorealism.

### What to carry forward from the references

- Visible, layered brushstrokes with an oil-paint / gouache-like impasto texture.
- A joyful split-complementary colour language: turquoise and deep teal shadows; apricot, honey-gold, and sunny yellow light; coral, raspberry, and rose accents; occasional moss and leaf green.
- Warm light that seems to glow through windows, trees, and paths.
- Cozy, imaginative places with plentiful environmental texture: flowers, cottages, cobbles, baking tools, garden objects, and gentle hills.
- A slightly dreamlike, nostalgic feeling, with colour doing as much storytelling as the objects.
- Animal characters in practical story-world clothes, posed with human-like intention but retaining their animal silhouettes and warmth.

### What to adapt for this book

- Keep **Milo’s silhouette, face, eyes, and envelope crisp and easy to read**. Do not let heavy texture obscure the protagonist or the page’s main action.
- Use fewer competing details than the references in any one scene. The book needs an immediate focal point for young children.
- Keep characters rounded, soft, affectionate, and non-threatening. No sharp claws, realistic teeth, or intense expressions.
- Favour late-spring Poppywick colours and flowering greenery rather than the winter snow shown in some references.
- Make the visual texture part of the paint surface, not visual noise or distortion.

## Visual palette

| Visual job | Primary colour family | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shadows and structure | Peacock teal, deep turquoise, blue-green | Cottage shade, clothing shadow, foliage depth, bicycle metal |
| Sunlight and warmth | Apricot, honey gold, butter yellow, peach | Sky glow, windows, paths, paper highlights, emotional warmth |
| Story accents | Poppy red, coral, raspberry | Milo’s satchel and bicycle, envelope seal, flowers, small focal details |
| Natural balance | Moss green, leaf green, soft cream | Trees, gardens, cottage walls, quiet text areas |

Avoid muddy grey-brown pages, neon colour, stark black outlines, or large areas of empty white.

## Image composition rules

- Every page is **5:4 landscape**. Compose for a 10-inch-wide by 8-inch-high frame from the start; never crop a portrait scene into landscape afterward.
- Use one strong foreground action, a small number of midground supports, and a simplified atmospheric background.
- Leave an intentionally quiet, low-detail area for later typography on every page. The final text block should never cover Milo’s face, a crucial hand/paw, the envelope, or a character’s eyes.
- Use warm light direction consistently within a page. Across the story, progress from fresh morning to golden late afternoon.
- Maintain clean scale and clear character separation in group scenes.

## Story-specific visual anchors

- **Milo:** His character-bible design is mandatory: ginger tabby; exactly three russet stripes on his forehead; leaf-green eyes; navy cap with gold paw badge; cream shirt; red diagonal satchel; red bicycle.
- **Mystery envelope:** Tiny cream landscape envelope with a round red wax seal. It is blank on the front until it is opened on the final page. The tiny ginger paw-print clue first appears in Page 4 of the book (Story Page 3).
- **Poppywick:** A village of rounded cottages, garden walls, flower boxes, apple trees, soft hills, the bakery, fountain crossroads, post office, and warm cobbled lanes.
- **Character continuity:** The complete authority is [`../Milo_the_Village_Postcat_Character_Bible.md`](../Milo_the_Village_Postcat_Character_Bible.md).

## Reference-image interpretation

The eight supplied images are treated as a **visual mood and technique reference**, not as scene content. Do not reproduce their people, elephant, dog, snow scenes, diner, signs, or exact compositions. Carry forward only the painterly texture, colour harmony, gentle golden light, and inviting story-world atmosphere.

## Stage 0 outcome

The book will be built as a 10 × 8 landscape, richly painterly children’s book. The next stage will convert this brief into one reusable master GPT-Image style prompt plus a concise exclusion list for consistent generation.
