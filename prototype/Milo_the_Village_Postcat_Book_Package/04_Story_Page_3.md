# Page 4 of 6 — Story Page 3: A Clue at the Fountain

## Page purpose

Let Milo’s friends join the mystery, then end on a visible clue. The page should have the energy of a small cooperative search while staying easy for a young child to scan.

## Story moment

Milo and his friends check the most likely places in the village. Nobody is waiting for the letter. Mouse spots something tiny beside the red seal.

## Characters present

- **Milo:** Centre of the group, holding the blank envelope level so Mouse can inspect it. His expression has shifted from puzzled to hopeful.
- **Bunny:** To Milo’s left, white rabbit in yellow apron. She has just returned from checking the bakery and holds a small empty bread basket.
- **Bear:** Behind Milo, crouched to the group’s height. Cocoa bear with green hat/orange flower and green apron; his empty watering can is set aside.
- **Mouse:** On the rim of the fish-shaped fountain, at envelope height. Grey mouse with violet scarf, one paw pointing at the clue.

## Illustration brief

### Composition

Set the image at Poppywick’s small crossroads fountain. Place Milo just left of centre, seated or kneeling at Mouse’s level. The envelope forms the visual centre. Bunny creates a bright yellow shape on the left; Bear creates a large soft green-and-brown shape behind; Mouse creates a small violet accent on the fountain rim at the right. This makes a single readable circle of attention around the letter.

### Foreground action

Mouse points to the very edge of the red wax seal. A tiny ginger paw print is just becoming visible there—small enough that a child will enjoy finding it, but clear enough to read immediately. Milo holds the envelope steady with both paws and looks exactly where Mouse points. Bunny leans in, ears alert. Bear crouches with one rounded paw beneath his chin, smiling softly.

### Midground and background

Show only hints of the group’s search: Bunny’s empty bread basket, Bear’s watering can, and the fountain itself. Three lanes lead away behind them, with a bakery sign on one and a garden gate on another. Include two birds on a signpost if desired. Do not show the final party or the other villagers yet.

### Light, palette, and mood

Warm, high midday light. The fountain water adds a quiet blue note; red seal, yellow apron, violet scarf, and green gardener’s clothes should remain distinct. The mood is a delighted “aha!” moment, never suspenseful.

### Complexity limit

**Medium.** Four characters, one focal object, three search clues, and a simple crossroads background. Every face must point toward the envelope; no unrelated action.

## Exact page text

Bunny checked the bakery.

Bear checked the garden.

Mouse checked the fountain.

But nobody was waiting for a tiny letter.

Then Mouse peeped, “Look!”

## Text treatment and placement

- Set the first four lines as narration in a calm pale area in the upper-left, clear of Bunny’s ears.
- Put “Look!” in one small speech bubble beside Mouse, with its tail pointing to her raised paw.
- The illustration—not the text—shows the paw-print clue.

## Continuity check

- The envelope’s front still has no name or address.
- The red wax seal now visibly includes the tiny ginger paw print; it must match Milo’s paw colour.
- Mouse’s violet scarf, Bunny’s yellow apron, and Bear’s green hat with orange flower are mandatory.
