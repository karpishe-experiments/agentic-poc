### Generation prompt

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: final cover artwork for a 10 × 8 inch landscape children’s book
Input image: supplied Milo character-reference sheet, used only as a character identity reference.

Create a 10 × 8 inch landscape cover illustration for the children’s book Milo the Village Postcat. Luminous hand-painted storybook art, richly layered gouache and controlled oil-paint impasto texture, soft painted-paper surface, peacock teal and turquoise shadows, apricot and honey-gold morning sunlight, warm cream cottages, leaf-green gardens, and small coral-red accents. Rounded, affectionate, child-friendly animal characters; no black outlines, no CGI, no photorealism.

Milo is the central hero: a small, round ginger tabby postcat with exactly three russet-brown forehead stripes, bright leaf-green eyes, pale cream muzzle/chest/paw tips, a navy-blue postman cap with gold paw badge, cream collared shirt, red canvas mail satchel, and long ringed tabby tail. Preserve the reference character exactly. He rides a tiny red bicycle with cream handlebar grips, wicker front basket, and brass bell. Milo rides gently toward the viewer on a curving cobbled lane, at a three-quarter angle, smiling softly and waving with one paw while the other steadies the handlebar. His satchel is full but closed; only a few colourful envelope corners peek out. His tail curls upward like a question mark.

Scene: early morning in Poppywick Village. One rounded warm-cream cottage and a bakery with a striped awning sit on the left; a small garden and apple tree sit on the right; gentle green hills appear in the distance. Bunny, a small white rabbit in a sunny yellow bakery apron, is a small midground cameo at the bakery door, holding a golden loaf and waving. Bear, a large cocoa-brown bear in a moss-green gardener’s hat with one orange flower and a dark-green gardening apron, is a small midground cameo watering daisies beneath the apple tree. Mouse, a tiny grey mouse in a violet scarf, is a tiny background cameo at an upstairs round window. Milo must remain the only sharp central focal character.

Composition: the cobbled lane begins in the bottom-centre foreground and curves into the village. Place Milo in the lower-centre/right third, large enough to read immediately. Keep the upper-left third as a calm, pale-blue and warm-gold sky area for title typography later. The title area must contain no faces, buildings, birds, letters, or important objects.

Lighting and mood: fresh sunlit morning, safe, inviting, full of gentle motion and neighbourly warmth.

Constraints: full-bleed 5:4 landscape composition; one foreground action; three small supporting cameos; clear character scale; intact Milo design; all clothing and props readable.

Avoid: printed text, title, captions, speech bubbles, readable signs, watermark, border, logo, signature, snow, people, dogs, elephants, modern vehicles, crowded scenes, loose letters flying through the frame, scary expressions, sharp claws, or any character competing with Milo.
```
