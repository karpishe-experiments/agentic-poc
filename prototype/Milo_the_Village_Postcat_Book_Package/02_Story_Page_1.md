# Page 2 of 6 — Story Page 1: Milo’s Morning Route

## Page purpose

Show what makes Milo lovable before the mystery begins: he notices the small, personal things that matter to his neighbours.

## Story moment

Mid-morning. Milo has nearly completed his usual route. His deliveries make Poppywick’s everyday places feel connected and cared for.

## Characters present

- **Milo:** Primary focus. Happy, careful, actively delivering a letter. Use all key character-bible traits and clothing.
- **Bunny:** Main recipient at the bakery. White fur, hazel eyes, yellow apron. She is delighted by her letter.
- **Bear:** Secondary recipient in his garden. Cocoa fur, green hat with orange flower, green overall apron. He is pleased but quiet.
- **Mouse:** Optional tiny background detail only, sitting on the low bakery step with her violet scarf. Do not give her a competing action.

## Illustration brief

### Composition

Landscape-like action within the portrait page: Milo is in the foreground-right, standing beside his parked red bicycle and handing an envelope to Bunny at the bakery counter window. The bakery occupies the left third; Bear’s garden is visible deeper down the lane on the right. The lane and garden wall lead the eye from Bunny to Bear.

### Foreground action

Milo offers Bunny a cream envelope using both paws, as though it is precious. Bunny receives it with both paws, leaning forward on her toes, one ear tilted playfully. Her bakery window displays just three simple things: two loaves, a cake with one candle, and a jar of jam. Milo’s expression is pleased and attentive, not exaggerated.

### Midground and background

Bear stands in his garden under an apple tree, reading a letter with a crescent smile. His watering can rests nearby. A red postbox, low stone wall, flower boxes, and a couple of apple blossoms establish the same Poppywick from the cover. If Mouse appears, she is a tiny, quiet observer on the bakery step—not a second focal point.

### Light, palette, and mood

Clean late-morning light with soft shadows. Keep the palette warm and calm: cream walls, yellow bakery accents, red bicycle/satchel, and garden greens. The emotion is everyday happiness.

### Complexity limit

**Medium-low.** One clear delivery exchange, one secondary delivery result, and a simple village setting. The scene should be legible at thumbnail size.

## Exact page text

Each morning, Milo’s bell went **ting!**

He carried birthday wishes, recipes, and hello-from-far-away letters.

Milo knew just where each one belonged.

And every doorstep felt brighter when he came by.

## Text treatment and placement

All text is narration—no speech bubbles. Set it in a calm cream panel or open pale wall area in the upper-right quadrant, away from Milo, Bunny, and the letter exchange.
