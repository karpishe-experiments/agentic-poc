# Guideline — Generating a Character List

**Applies to:** Artifact 03 — Character List  
**Purpose:** Create a lean, story-relevant cast record before visual style or detailed character design.

## Required inputs

- Approved Book Concept Brief
- Approved Story Outline
- Mandatory audience age range

## Method

1. Extract every character required by the outline’s causal story beats.
2. Include only main, recurring, or scene-critical characters. Group incidental background people where individual identity does not affect the story.
3. Record each character’s narrative function, brief presence, connection to the protagonist, likely recurrence, and intentionally unresolved details.
4. Apply representation and age-appropriateness constraints from the approved foundation artifacts.
5. Keep visual details, fashion, precise physical traits, mannerisms, names, and image-generation direction out of this artifact unless already locked upstream.
6. Present the result as **Draft awaiting approval**. Do not treat it as approved until the user explicitly approves it.

## Controlled template

```markdown
# Character List — *[Book Title]*

**Artifact:** 03 — Character List  
**Status:** Draft awaiting approval  
**Audience:** [age or age range]  
**Based on:** [approved upstream artifacts]

## Purpose

[One sentence.]

## Character list

| Character | Story role | Short personality / presence | Relationship to protagonist | Recurrence | Details intentionally open |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |

## Cast constraints

- ...

## Decisions deferred

- ...
```

## Quality checks

- Every character serves a concrete story function.
- The cast is neither missing a necessary role nor padded with decorative named characters.
- The audience age range informs the intensity, emotional complexity, and antagonist role.
- Open decisions are clearly separated from approved facts.
- The artifact does not pre-empt the later Visual Style Package or Individual Character Packages.

## Common failures

- Turning the list into a full character bible.
- Adding visual design decisions before the visual style is approved.
- Introducing characters who do not affect a story beat.
- Treating a draft as approved without explicit user confirmation.
