# Guideline — Generating a Story Outline

## Purpose

Turn an approved Book Concept Brief into a short, tellable story skeleton. It should read like someone quickly explaining the entire book: who the main people are, where the story broadly happens, what starts the action, what the main actions are, and how it resolves.

This is not the manuscript. It contains no page-by-page prose, detailed scene direction, character appearance, or visual design.

## Required inputs

- Approved Book Concept Brief.
- Relevant approved creator decisions from chat context.

## Required artifact

```markdown
# Story Outline — [Working Title]

**Status:** Draft awaiting approval
**Based on:** [approved Book Concept Brief]

## One-Paragraph Story Summary
## Main and Supporting Character Shortlist
| Name or role | Narrative role | Relationship / function | Details intentionally open |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
## Key Places Mentioned
| Place | Story purpose | Details intentionally open |
| --- | --- | --- |
## Story Arc
1. Opening situation
2. Inciting action
3. Escalation / main actions
4. Climax or decisive choice
5. Resolution
## Theme and Emotional Arc
## Intentionally Deferred Decisions
## Approval Questions
```

## Method

1. Preserve the approved premise, audience, theme, and constraints.
2. Write a concise causal storyline: setup → change → response → challenge → meaningful action → resolution.
3. Include a short main/supporting character shortlist and a short list of key places inside this artifact. These are overview material only; they are not separate list artifacts.
4. Keep identity, appearance, age, wardrobe, visual style, detailed setting design, dialogue, and page structure open unless the user already fixed them.
5. Ask questions or offer options only where a missing decision changes the narrative direction, then present a draft for approval.

## Quality gate

- The full story is understandable in a short read.
- Main and supporting roles, key places, and central action are clear.
- The protagonist has meaningful agency.
- The artifact does not duplicate the later Character List, Location List, or manuscript.
