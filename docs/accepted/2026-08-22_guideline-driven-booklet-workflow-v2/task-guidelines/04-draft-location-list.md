# Guideline — Generating a Draft Location List

## Purpose

Create a working list of the places implied by the approved Story Outline before the manuscript is written. It gives the writer and creator a shared memory of the story world without becoming a final location specification, world guide, or illustration brief.

## Required inputs

- Approved Book Concept Brief and Story Outline.
- Relevant user decisions from chat context.

## Required artifact

```markdown
# Draft Location List — [Working Title]

**Status:** Draft awaiting approval
**Based on:** [approved concept brief and story outline]

| Location title | Story purpose | Known facts | Likely scenes or actions | Open questions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## World and Safety Notes
## Questions for Manuscript Development
```

## Method and rules

1. Start with only the places needed by the outline's story beats.
2. Give every place a stable title, even when it is functional, such as `Neighbourhood Park` or `Bus Stop`.
3. Record the basic story purpose and known facts, but keep layout, architecture, time of day, weather, lighting, camera angle, and visual style open.
4. Do not build a broad world encyclopedia or add locations without a narrative use.
5. This draft is revised by the manuscript process and is replaced by the Final Location List after manuscript approval.

## Quality gate

- Every listed place has a story use.
- The list is sufficient to support manuscript development without deciding later visual-reference work.
