# Guideline — Generating a Final Page Bible

## Purpose

Create the final per-physical-page production source after the book's layout is approved. The Page Bible specifies exactly what each page must depict and say, including action, composition, text, speech/thought bubbles, and all panel/image requirements. It does not repeat character or location descriptions; approved sheets are the visual authority for those.

## Required inputs

- Approved Page-by-Page Manuscript and Pagination & Layout Plan.
- Approved Visual Style Package, Final Character List, Final Location List, and all required Character and Location Sheet Packages.

## Required artifact

```markdown
# Final Page Bible — [Working Title]

**Status:** Draft awaiting approval
**Based on:** [approved source versions]

## Global Production Rules

## Physical Page [number] — [role/title]
- **Layout and panel/image structure:**
- **Story action and emotional beat:**
- **Composition definition:**
- **Required character-sheet references:**
- **Required location-sheet references:**
- **Exact text plan:**

| Item | Exact wording | Form | Speaker | Panel/image | Placement | Typography / bubble treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
```

## Method and rules

1. Use the Pagination & Layout Plan as the authoritative physical-page, panel/image, aspect-ratio, typography, and bubble-system source.
2. Use the manuscript as the authoritative story action and dialogue source. Resolve any mismatch through a revision to the owning artifact; never improvise around a conflict.
3. For every physical page, state exact action, required visual composition, text, bubble form, speaker, hierarchy, placement, and the specific approved sheet references.
4. Explicitly mark pages or panels with no text. Include cover, title/legal pages, end matter, and back cover where present.
5. Do not repeat or reinterpret character appearance or location design. Point to the approved sheet IDs instead.
6. Do not generate a final page until the relevant Bible entry is approved.

## Quality gate

- Every physical page and each of its panels/images has complete production instructions.
- Every readable word and bubble treatment is exact and approved.
- The layout can be generated without inventing missing text, action, or composition.
