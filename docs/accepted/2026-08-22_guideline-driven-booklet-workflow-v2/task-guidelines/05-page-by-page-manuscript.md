# Guideline — Generating a Page-by-Page Manuscript

## Purpose

Write the complete non-illustrated storybook text from the approved narrative foundation and draft lists. It is the full scenario of the book: page by page and scene by scene, with what happens, the setting and action details required for the story, narration, and dialogue. A reader should be able to read it as fiction and know the exact story without seeing illustrations.

The manuscript is not a visual-style document, physical layout plan, Page Bible, or image prompt.

## Required inputs

- Approved Book Concept Brief and Story Outline.
- Approved Draft Character List and Draft Location List.
- Relevant user decisions from chat context.

## Required artifact

```markdown
# Page-by-Page Manuscript — [Working Title]

**Status:** Draft awaiting approval
**Based on:** [approved narrative artifacts]

## Story Continuity Notes

## Manuscript Pages
### Manuscript Page [number] — [beat title]
- **Scene and story action:**
- **Characters present:**
- **Location:**
- **Narration and dialogue:**

## Character and Location Expansion Notes
## Open Questions
## Approval Gate
```

## Method and rules

1. Preserve the approved story skeleton while expanding it into complete, child-facing prose.
2. Give each manuscript page or scene a clear narrative job, required action, characters, location, narration, and necessary dialogue.
3. Include story-relevant scene and action detail so the later production process understands what must occur, but do not prescribe camera, illustration composition, speech-bubble design, font, aspect ratio, or image prompts.
4. Add new required characters and locations to the working lists as the manuscript makes them necessary. Record expanded facts as manuscript notes, not as a separate final artifact before approval.
5. Keep the text readable as a non-illustrated storybook. The prose must be complete, causal, age-appropriate, and emotionally coherent.
6. After explicit manuscript approval, hand its confirmed cast and places to the Final Character List and Final Location List specialists.

## Quality gate

- Every page/scene contains complete readable story text.
- The story can be understood without pictures.
- Character and location requirements are explicit enough to create final lists.
- No later design or layout decision has been prematurely locked.
