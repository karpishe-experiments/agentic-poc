# Guideline — Generating a Draft Character List

## Purpose

Create a working cast record from the approved Story Outline before the manuscript is written. It is a practical memory aid and starting point, not a final character specification or an image-generation brief.

## Required inputs

- Approved Book Concept Brief and Story Outline.
- Relevant user decisions from chat context.

## Required artifact

```markdown
# Draft Character List — [Working Title]

**Status:** Draft awaiting approval
**Based on:** [approved concept brief and story outline]

| Name or role | Narrative function | Main / supporting / incidental | Known relationship | Known facts | Open questions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Cast Notes
## Representation and Safety Requirements
## Questions for Manuscript Development
```

## Method and rules

1. Expand the outline's shortlist only as far as the current story needs.
2. Use a name when known; otherwise use a stable role such as `Passer-by 01`.
3. Record confirmed facts and open questions separately. Do not invent physical description, age, clothing, style, or image prompts.
4. Include incidental roles only when they matter to the manuscript; do not turn every background figure into a character record.
5. This draft is revised by the manuscript process and is replaced by the Final Character List after manuscript approval.

## Quality gate

- Every named or story-critical role has a clear purpose.
- No visual-character design is prematurely locked.
- The list supports manuscript writing without pretending to be the final source of production truth.
